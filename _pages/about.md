<span class="anchor" id="about-me"></span>

I am a first-year Ph.D. student (2026.09–present) in the Department of Automation, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), co-supervised by [Zhaoyang Lyu](https://zhaoyanglyu.github.io) and [Dahua Lin](http://dahua.site). I am also a research intern at the Physical Intelligence Center, [Shanghai AI Laboratory](https://www.shlab.org.cn).

My research interests lie in **embodied intelligence**, particularly **world modeling** and **dexterous manipulation**.
